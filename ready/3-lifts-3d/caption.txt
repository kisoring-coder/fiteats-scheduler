No time for the gym? You only need these 3 lifts. 🏋️

1. Barbell hang clean & press: forearms, traps, shoulders, triceps, upper back, plus glutes and legs to drive the bar up.
2. Deadlift: hamstrings, glutes, lower back, lats, traps and grip.
3. Barbell back squat: quads, glutes, inner thighs, hamstrings and core.

Got 5 more minutes? Add push-ups for your chest.

Learn the form with a light bar first, then add weight slowly.
