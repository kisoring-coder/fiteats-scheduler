Idli and dosa aren't the enemy. The problem is how little protein they give you. 🍽️

4 idlis = only about 10 g of protein. Two simple fixes for a high-protein South Indian breakfast:

✅ Grind soaked chana dal or moong dal and add it to your idli batter
✅ Swap tomato chutney for groundnut chutney: a handful of peanuts adds about 5 g of protein
